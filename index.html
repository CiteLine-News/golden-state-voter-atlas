Beyond Search

What makes this more useful than a plain search page

Source traceability

Every summary points back to campaign or state election material.

Instead of a vague result list, each candidate profile includes direct links to policy pages, voter-guide text, and statewide ballot records.

Difference detection

The compare desk pulls out overlap, contrast, and signature proposals.

That makes it easier to see when candidates talk about the same issue but offer very different governing approaches.

Research workflow

Bookmarks, shareable filters, and copyable briefs help you keep momentum.

You can build a shortlist, save a view in the URL, and export a concise comparison note for later discussion.

Methodology

This first version focuses on major active campaigns with enough public policy material to compare. It uses a curated April 15, 2026 snapshot rather than the full certified field.

Use Note

This page is for research and comparison, not endorsement. Always verify live developments through the linked official sources before making a final voting decision.
